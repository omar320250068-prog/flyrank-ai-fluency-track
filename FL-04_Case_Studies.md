## Case 1 — LIVE project: numbers → monthly report (What I'm building)

**The problem**
Monthly reports took too long and I never fully trusted the numbers. Figures sat scattered across sheets, totals disagreed, and finishing meant re-checking everything myself.

**What I did, and what I decided**
The core decision: **verify, then draft.** The workflow reconciles every number against its source before a word of the report is written, and anything it can't confirm gets flagged as UNVERIFIED instead of guessed. I decided what stays human: I review the flags and the final draft before anything ships. The AI never decides what's true.

**What came of it**
A live, try-it-now workflow (this internship's build). "Done well" is measurable: every number traceable to a source, the company template filled, my full review under ten minutes, zero fabricated figures. That's the proof on the Home page.

**CTA:** TRY THE LIVE PROJECT →

## Case 2 — the saving automation (a real deliverable I already ship)

**The problem**
A repetitive weekly task burned hours: pulling data from several sources, cleaning it, and getting a usable file out the other side.

**What I did, and what I decided**
I built an automation (spreadsheet + script) that merges, cleans, and cross-checks the data in one run. The decision I kept: a manual spot-check still happens before the numbers leave my desk — automation, not blindness.

**What came of it**
The task that used to eat hours of my week now runs in minutes, every week, and rework from errors dropped. It's been running since; it doesn't need me to babysit it.

**CTA:** → back to the live project
